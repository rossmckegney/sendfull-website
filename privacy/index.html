Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains how Sendfull LLC (“Sendfull”) handles information on sendfull.com, including the automation diagnostic and the contact form.

1. What we collect

When you take the diagnostic, you give us:

  • Your name
  • Your work email address
  • Optionally: your company and role
  • The initiative you are assessing, and an optional one-sentence description
  • Your responses to the diagnostic questions

While a run is in progress, a draft of those details and answers is saved in this browser’s localStorage so you can resume. That draft is not sent to Sendfull. When you finish, the draft is removed and a copy of the finished run — your details, your answers, and the result — is saved in localStorage on that device. Clearing this site’s data in your browser removes it.

Finishing also emails a summary to the Sendfull team. The summary includes your name, email, any company and role you gave, the initiative, your scores, and the recommended next step. It does not include a copy of every answer. The site does not email that summary to you.

Google Analytics records pages visited on the whole site, and diagnostic events such as starting, resuming, submitting your details, answering a question, completing, and re-taking. Google receives that usage data, which can include an approximate location.

The workshop, diagnostic, privacy, and terms pages load the Sora and Inter typefaces from Google Fonts. That request sends your IP address to Google. Your diagnostic answers are not part of it.

The contact form collects the email address and message you type.

2. What we do with it

We use this information to:

  • Email the diagnostic summary to hello@sendfull.com, and keep the in-progress draft and the finished run in your browser, as described above
  • Reply to messages sent through the contact form
  • Improve the diagnostic based on the responses we receive
  • Occasionally email you about related Sendfull work, such as the book What Your Machines Should Do or upcoming workshops

The address you enter is included so we can reply. We do not automatically send you a copy of your diagnostic results. You can opt out of follow-up emails at any time by replying to any email from us or by contacting hello@sendfull.com.

3. What we do not do with it

We do not sell your information. We do not give it to other companies so they can market their own products to you.

4. Services we use

  • Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-5BM6XJ7WQD), loaded with gtag.js on every page, for the page views and diagnostic events above.
  • Formspree (form formspree.io/f/mykdjprq) delivers the diagnostic summary and contact-form messages to hello@sendfull.com. Formspree processes those fields in order to send that email.
  • Google Fonts serves Sora and Inter on the workshop, diagnostic, privacy, and terms pages.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site.

These services process information only as needed to provide their function.

5. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • See what information we have about you
  • Correct or delete that information
  • Stop sending you follow-up emails

We will respond within 45 days. To make any of these requests, email hello@sendfull.com.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version will always be at this URL, with the “Last updated” date reflecting the most recent change.
